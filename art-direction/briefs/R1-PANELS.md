## Selection gates (per panel, judged by the orchestrator from the files)

- R-a No text: any letter, numeral, symbol, logo, label, or UI element rejects (VISUAL_STRATEGY 3.7).
- R-g On master: the drift check (VISUAL_STRATEGY 2.3 rule 8) answers yes; no dominant hue outside the palette families plus neutral metal and wood.
- Content match: the panel shows the subjects its VISUAL_STRATEGY 2.2 cell names, and nothing the cell does not name as a dominant element. This is the panel level read, in the same spirit as the R0 session's R-m gate.

Choosing among passing candidates for one panel: the one whose named subjects read most clearly at thumbnail size (256 px wide), then the one with the fewest off brief elements. If neither candidate for a panel passes, VISUAL_STRATEGY 3.7 allows one more round with one brief change; a panel never blocks the session for more than two rounds, and the fallback is the panel's own description on the v1 master.
