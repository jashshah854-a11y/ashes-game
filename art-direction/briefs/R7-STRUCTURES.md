## Selection gates (VISUAL_STRATEGY 3.7, all apply to board pieces)

- R-a No text: any letter, numeral, symbol, logo, label, or UI element rejects.
- R-b Clean framing: single subject, fully in frame, at least 8 percent margin on each side; border strip (outer 10 percent) luma standard deviation 6 or less.
- R-c Rig match: view from about 45 degrees above, near orthographic, shadow falls lower right.
- R-d Silhouette at final size: the silhouette battery (Step 22b tool) against the current target or blockout of every other board family, 100 percent or reject. Where a family has no selected hero yet, its current target is its master panel silhouette; after all R6 and R7 selections, R-d reruns across the full selected set (DECISIONS item 38 safeguard 5).
- R-e Tintable surfaces: pixels within 20 degrees hue of the Vessari blue and saturation above 0.35 cover 20 to 60 percent of the subject area, in 3 or fewer connected regions, each at least 5 percent of the subject.
- R-f Buildable: every part is a primitive, a bevelled block, a lathe, or a simple sculpt; part count within the section 4.3 budget; nothing melted, impossible, or ambiguous.
- R-g On master: the drift check (VISUAL_STRATEGY 2.3 rule 8) answers same world yes; no dominant hue outside the palette families plus neutral metal and wood.
- Choosing among passing candidates: Claude Design's eye, on the section 3.3 silhouette key read at final size (the family's plain name must read at 56 px, 64 for S-CAP).
