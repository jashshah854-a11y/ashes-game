# Ashes of Empires, Backlog

Honest list of what is stubbed, simplified, or missing. If it is not in the
game, it is written here. Nothing in this file is secretly faked in the code.

## Stubbed or simplified (current, after the Cycle 6 reconciliation)

- Battle model: simultaneous damage rounds, damage soaks into the first unit of a stack (by unit id order now, deterministically), no terrain, no fortification bonus, no retreat. Supply penalties do apply to attack and defense through unitStats. Simultaneous resolution itself shipped in Cycle 6 step 2 (see Done below).
- Tick order: income, then production lines, then factories (lines are paid first since Cycle 6 step 4), then construction, supply, the AI turn, and movement. The player acts before the tick; the AI decides inside it, after those phases, except research, which it decides at the start of the tick like a player's click (since Cycle 6 step 4, removing a one day Drone Gen 2 head start). Its factory, rail, production, and movement decisions still run mid tick: it sees one more day of income when it builds factories and rail, and its production orders start a day later than a player's. Moving the whole AI turn to the start of the tick was measured and rejected as one slice (a passive player then held to day 64 instead of 36 and survived 19 of 200 seeds).
- AI: scripted rules only. Since Cycle 3 it decides per battle group with the shared stackPower estimate, needs 1.5x superiority to attack a defended province, will not attack while out of supply, builds rail toward its front every few days, and waits out a 10-day mobilization. Since Cycle 5 step 4 it holds a garrison of the first 3 idle units on its own capital (they receive no orders; units beyond 3 advance normally). It never plans beyond the next province, never builds drones vs infantry by strategy, and its research is a timer, not a choice.
- Balance state after the Cycle 6 reconciliation (measured with the committed tools/balance.js, see CYCLE_LOG; reproduce with `node tools/balance.js`): on the 8 standard seeds a passive player falls on day 36 on all 8; the intended path (sensible play that builds factories toward the next line) wins 8 of 8 on days 22 to 48 with end strength 122.2 to 248.6 percent of the 72 starting strength, lines 3 and 4 building on every seed; a reckless player loses 8 of 8 on day 36. Over seeds 1 to 200: passive and reckless never survive, the factory path wins 200 of 200 (median day 34). The Cycle 5 sensible script that never builds a factory is now a report, not a ruling path: 7 of 8 standard seeds (loses seed 1 on day 78 to a Dominion running 4 lines), 193 of 200. Superseded measurements, kept for history: Cycle 5's days 43 to 52 (an uncommitted script, factories paid first, sequential battles) and Vector's step 4 day 25 on all 8 (another uncommitted script that rationed factory builds, factories paid first). The factory path may now be too strong; that calibration is Jash's call (DECISIONS_FOR_JASH.md). Tuning belongs to later cycles, especially once fortifications exist.
- Economy: one equipment stockpile and one resource (steel). No manpower, no fuel, no unit upkeep, no partial unit reinforcement. Exact factory economics: building a factory level costs equipment (40 times the target level); running factories consume steel (2 per level per day) and produce equipment (5 per level per day). Since Cycle 6 step 4 production lines are paid before factories each day, and a factory level the leftover steel cannot feed sits idle; the nation card shows how many levels are idle and why. Known limit: equipment buys nothing but factories, so once every line is open it piles up unused (over 5000 in long games). Since Cycle 5 step 1, units are built on production lines over days for steel and deploy at the capital; equipment no longer buys units and funds factory construction only. The Cycle 5 judge ruled that factories must gate production line count (not build speed), acceptable as factory-only currency for one more cycle only; that gating shipped in the sim in Cycle 6 step 1 (see below), and the one more cycle clock on equipment as a factory only currency is now running.
- Research: exactly one item (Drone Gen 2). Points accrue at a flat daily rate with no labs, no choices, no EW tree.
- Map: the rules run on an explicit typed-edge graph since Cycle 3 (land and river edges; coastal is a province flag). The renderer still draws a flat 10 by 7 grid with 4-direction movement, province names are syllable generated, terrain types have no combat effect yet, no sea provinces, no province shapes.
- UI: full re-render of the map every tick, no animations, no sound, no save or load, no minimap, no tooltips beyond the hint text. Selected unit orders show in the unit card only, not as arrows on the map.

- Production lines (Cycle 5, all 4 steps done, judge PASS): each nation has 2 lines (line 1 infantry, line 2 drone at start), a queue cap of 9 per line, no way to cancel or refund a queued order, and no way to pause a line (cancel and pause are parked pending Jash's call, see DECISIONS_FOR_JASH.md); retooling scraps work in progress with no steel refund, and the panel now states the steel that will be lost before the player acts. The production panel (step 2) shows each line, its template, queue, days remaining, and steel drain, and the unit card shows unit cost; the old train buttons are gone. Line count is gated by factories in the sim since Cycle 6 step 1 (thresholds and rules below), and the panel shows the locked slots with their unlock conditions since Cycle 6 step 3 (see below). Simultaneous battle resolution shipped in Cycle 6 step 2 (see below). Cycle 6 step 4 (tests and balance) is done; only step 5 (judge) remains in Cycle 6.

## Done since Iteration 1 (kept honest, limits noted)

- Balance harness (Cycle 6 step 4, ported in the reconciliation): tools/balance.js plays passive, sensible without factories (report only), sensible with factories, and reckless campaigns through public commands only, checks Jash's ruling with exit code 1 on failure, and runs seed ranges for mass testing (300 campaigns in about 2.5 seconds). Its sensible script is rebuilt from the log's definition because the Cycle 5 script was never committed; validated against the Cycle 5 sim, it wins faster than the lost original (see CYCLE_LOG).

- Simultaneous battle resolution (Cycle 6 step 2, sim plus one preview line): the movement phase now computes every engagement from a start of phase snapshot on unit copies, then applies all results at once. No nation's groups resolve first; each engagement gets its own seeded rng and canonically sorted unit lists, so reversing the unit array gives identical results (verified by the orchestrator). Opposing groups crossing the same edge in opposite directions meet in a road battle where both sides deal damage with their attack power; the winner marches on if its target is not held by staying enemy units. A province's snapshot occupants defend it even if they had orders elsewhere (their orders are cancelled for the tick), all attacking groups against one province combine into one battle, and opposing groups converging on one empty province clash there. The unit card preview keeps the province battle numbers and adds the meeting battle numbers (your attack vs their attack). The formal tests for order reversal and the edge swap plus the full balance rerun shipped in step 4 (suite 39 at the time, 42 after the reconciliation added a test that ordered defenders still defend). Only the judge (step 5) remains. The locked slot panel shipped in step 3 (see below).

- Factory gated line count (Cycle 6 step 1, sim only): each nation still starts with exactly 2 lines. Line 3 unlocks at 16 total factory levels and line 4 at 24 (one tunable config, CFG.lineUnlockFactoryLevels [0, 0, 16, 24], hard cap CFG.maxProductionLines 4). An unlock is permanent once reached. Queueing or retooling a locked line is rejected with a reason naming how many more factory levels are needed, the threshold, and the current total. The AI syncs and queues on extra lines through the identical path and costs (verified: at 16 enemy factory levels, one real tick left the AI with line 3 open and an order queued on it). The panel side shipped in step 3 (below), and the factory card states the thresholds, the live total, and permanence. Formal tests and the balance rerun shipped in step 4, which also fixed the factory path itself: with factories paid before lines it was a steel trap, and lines are now paid first. Only the judge (step 5) remains.

- Locked slot panel (Cycle 6 step 3, UI only): the production panel renders every locked slot below the live lines as a dimmed, dashed row with no buttons, titled "Line N: Locked" and stating the exact condition in plain words from the sim's own numbers, for example "Line 3 locked. Build 9 more factory levels anywhere to unlock it (needs 16 total factory levels, you have 7)." plus a note that the line appears on its own when the total is reached. renderProduction syncs the player's lines first, so an earned slot appears the moment factories reach the threshold rather than after the next production phase. Verified in real headless Chromium against the repo index.html (DOM assertions on both locked rows, their exact text against the live factory total, and zero buttons, plus a screenshot). One honest gap: the unlock transition itself was not driven end to end through pure UI clicks in that session (an unattended clicker lost its capital on day 36 before enough equipment accrued); the transition is verified at sim level from step 1 and by the formal tests in step 4. The locked slot browser render check was repeated and passed in step 4 (2 live rows, 2 locked rows, exact condition text against the live factory total, zero buttons in the locked rows, screenshot inspected). The transition gap was closed in the reconciliation: real factory clicks while paused counted the row down to 1, then line 3 opened with working buttons.


- Production lines and unit costs (Cycle 5 step 1): lines assigned a template, steel cost per unit paid as a daily drain over build days (infantry 30 steel over 6 days, drone 15 steel over 3 days, 5 steel per day per working line), finished units deploy at the capital, and the AI orders through the identical lines and costs. A day the stockpile cannot pay the drain is a stalled day with no progress, so rail building and unit production compete for the same steel.
- Production tests and balance rerun (Cycle 5 step 3): 10 Opus authored tests pin budget parity between player and AI, queue completion timing (infantry on the 6th funded day for 30 steel, drone on the 3rd for 15, sequential queues, cap of 9), and steel starvation where a rail purchase up front stalls a production line with no progress and no charge. Balance rerun on the unchanged sim: passive player falls on day 36 on all 8 prescribed seeds, scripted sensible player survives 8 of 8, all by capital capture on day 28. Suite is now 30 tests, all passing. (The day 28 capture result was superseded in step 4: the Dominion capital garrison fix slowed sensible wins to days 43 to 52, still 8 of 8.)
- Balance ruling checks, garrison fix, and judge pass (Cycle 5 step 4): Jash's ruling checks run on all 8 seeds. The reckless check failed pre-fix (reckless won 5 of 8 by walking into a Khessograd the AI had abandoned on day 1) and passed post-fix (reckless loses 8 of 8); the sensible check passed before and after (8 of 8 capital captures through the public command path, end strength 81.9 to 144.4 percent of starting 72, floor is 20 percent). Fix: the Dominion holds its first 3 idle capital units as a garrison. Judge verdict PASS with all fix-now flags closed in the same step: garrison and retool tests (suite now 32, all passing), the retool note stating the steel at risk, and a real headless Chromium render and interaction check of the production panel and unit cost card.
- Production panel (Cycle 5 step 2): a Production card lists both player lines with template, full cost and build days, queue count against the cap, days paid and days left on the unit in progress, steel drain per day, a stalled warning when a day goes unpaid, and queue and retool buttons per line. A summary line shows total production drain and queued units, and the selected unit card now shows its build cost. The old province card train buttons were removed.
- Rail network and supply (Cycle 3): rail edges buildable for steel over days, supply computed over the owner's rail net within 8 hops, out-of-supply penalties and attrition as named, visible modifiers, captured rail converts over 4 days before it feeds the capturer. Limits: no rail levels or capacity, no supply effect on the economy, hover route highlighting not built (the inspector states the reason instead).

## Bible v1 systems not yet started (future cycles, Jash gated)

- Fortifications (construction verb, and a defense bonus in combat).
- Navy at theater scale: coastal control, river crossings, blockade pressure on the economy.
- Air layer beyond drone wings as ground-adjacent units: interception and the drone cost-exchange scoreboard (who bleeds money faster), which is a signature mechanic and deserves its own cycle.
- EW arms race: jamming generations countering drone generations in the research tree.
- Intel and recon: fog of war, drone recon lifting it, finding soft spots.
- Production line efficiency over time (lines exist since Cycle 5 step 1 and can be retooled; output does not ramp with experience yet).
- Lore: nation backstories in an in-game codex, event cards that fire as the campaign unfolds, named characters or grudges. Current lore is names only.
- Multiple AI rivals and a second theater.
- Save and load, campaign scoring screen, difficulty settings.

## Explicitly out of scope for v1 (per the bible, do not build)

World map, hundreds of nations, deep diplomacy, political parties, global trade market, naval invasions, multiple playable nations.
