# Ashes of Empires, Backlog

Honest list of what is stubbed, simplified, or missing. If it is not in the
game, it is written here. Nothing in this file is secretly faked in the code.

## Stubbed or simplified (current, after Cycle 9)

- Battle model: simultaneous damage rounds, damage soaks into the first unit of a stack (by unit id order now, deterministically), no terrain, no retreat. Fortifications give defenders a named defense bonus since Cycle 8 (see Done). Supply penalties do apply to attack and defense through unitStats. Simultaneous resolution itself shipped in Cycle 6 step 2 (see Done below).
- Tick order: at the start of the tick the Dominion decides research, factory growth, rail, fortification, and production orders, the same moment a player's clicks land (research since Cycle 6 step 4, the rest since Cycle 9); then income, production lines, factories (lines are paid first since Cycle 6 step 4), construction, supply, the Dominion's movement orders, and movement. Its movement orders are still decided after the supply phase, and that is a remaining gap, not a necessity: a new Dominion unit can march on the tick it is built (all 56 built in the 8 passive games did) while a player's new unit waits a tick, and its 1.5x attack estimate reads today's supply penalties and today's finished walls while the player's preview is a step behind. The Cycle 9 judge measured a variant with the whole movement decision at the start of the tick (passive day 30 on all 8 standard seeds, every ruling check passing over 200 seeds); it is the first measured slice of Cycle 10. A parity test pins that the same production, factory, and rail order from both sides runs on the same days. Moving all of it in one slice had held a passive player to day 64; moving rail alone held passive to day 69 until the Dominion's rail chooser was made to build its Spine first (Cycle 9 traced that the old chooser bridged its own Spine across the river only by luck).
- Same tick double capital fall: if both capitals change hands in one movement phase, the Dominion wins, only because captures apply in province id order and the Vessari capital (p0_3) sorts first. Never seen in the harness runs; the real rule is parked for Jash.
- Attack preview supply numbers are one supply step stale: the preview reads the penalty units carry now, and the supply phase of the next tick applies one more day of penalty or recovery before the battle is fought.
- AI: scripted rules only. Since Cycle 3 it decides per battle group with the shared stackPower estimate, needs 1.5x superiority to attack a defended province, will not attack while out of supply, builds rail toward its front every few days, and waits out a 10-day mobilization. Since Cycle 5 step 4 it holds a garrison of the first 3 idle units on its own capital (they receive no orders; units beyond 3 advance normally). It never plans beyond the next province, never builds drones vs infantry by strategy, and its research is a timer, not a choice.
- Balance state after Cycle 9 (measured with the committed tools/balance.js; reproduce with `node tools/balance.js`, reference output in tools/baseline-cycle9.txt): on the 8 standard seeds a passive player falls on day 34 on all 8; both reckless scripts lose 8 of 8 (day 34 and 37); the ruling paths win 8 of 8 above the floor: sensible with factories on days 34 to 106 (end strength 250.0 to 336.1 percent of the 72 starting strength), the same plus walls (fortress) on days 34 to 58, rationed on days 24 to 49 (152.8 to 236.1). Over seeds 1 to 200: passive and both reckless scripts never survive; factories and fortress win 200 of 200 (median days 55 and 46); rationed 199 (seed 104 lost to its own rail rule, traced in CYCLE_LOG). The Cycle 5 sensible script that never builds a factory is a report, not a ruling path: 8 of 8 standard seeds on day 46, 196 of 200. Most wars end as a siege of a walled Khessograd decided by massing about 1.5x its defense (parked for Jash). Superseded measurements, kept for history: Cycle 5's days 43 to 52, Vector's step 4 day 25 on all 8, and tools/baseline-cycle6.txt (no walls) and tools/baseline-cycle8.txt (the Dominion still deciding mid tick).
- Economy: one equipment stockpile and one resource (steel). No manpower, no fuel, no unit upkeep, no partial unit reinforcement. Exact factory economics: building a factory level costs equipment (40 times the target level); running factories consume steel (2 per level per day) and produce equipment (5 per level per day). Since Cycle 6 step 4 production lines are paid before factories each day, and a factory level the leftover steel cannot feed sits idle; the nation card shows how many levels are idle and why. Known limits: equipment buys nothing but factories, so once every line is open it piles up unused (over 5000 in long games); the Cycle 5 judge's one more cycle clock on that ran out with Cycle 6 and was re-dated to Cycle 8, where fortifications paid in equipment shipped: a player who fortifies ends campaigns with at most 3360 equipment over 200 seeds (Cycle 9). A player who never fortifies, and the Dominion (which walls only its capital and its river Spine province), still pile it up, the Dominion up to about 14000 in long campaigns (13960 against the rationed script over 200 seeds, Cycle 9). Factories cannot be paused and eat every steel the lines leave, so on the factory path rail goes unbuilt: over the 8 standard seeds the factories profile lays 0 to 7 rail edges per campaign and spends 21 to 97 days at 0 or 1 steel (Cycle 9; 21 to 83 in Cycle 8 and 8 to 43 before walls made campaigns longer), the fortress profile 0 to 7 edges and 21 to 52 days, against 9 to 32 edges for rationed and 30 to 40 for the no factory script, neither ever dry (the order of rail against factories is parked for Jash). Since Cycle 5 step 1, units are built on production lines over days for steel and deploy at the capital; equipment no longer buys units and funds factory construction only. The Cycle 5 judge ruled that factories must gate production line count (not build speed), acceptable as factory-only currency for one more cycle only; that gating shipped in the sim in Cycle 6 step 1 (see below); since Cycle 8 equipment also buys fortifications.
- Research: exactly one item (Drone Gen 2). Points accrue at a flat daily rate with no labs, no choices, no EW tree.
- Map: the rules run on an explicit typed-edge graph since Cycle 3 (land and river edges; coastal is a province flag). The renderer still draws a flat 10 by 7 grid with 4-direction movement, province names are syllable generated and repeat (a legibility defect found by the Cycle 9 judge: on seed 104 two provinces are named Vesford, one of them the river Spine province, and four are named Vorkar, so a log line like "capture Vesford" is ambiguous; names come from the seeded generator, so a fix changes every map's names and needs its own measured slice), terrain types have no combat effect yet, no sea provinces, no province shapes.
- UI: full re-render of the map every tick, no animations, no sound, no save or load, no minimap, no tooltips beyond the hint text. Selected unit orders show in the unit card only, not as arrows on the map.

- Production lines (Cycle 5, all 4 steps done, judge PASS): each nation has 2 lines (line 1 infantry, line 2 drone at start), a queue cap of 9 per line, no way to cancel or refund a queued order, and no way to pause a line (cancel and pause are parked pending Jash's call, see DECISIONS_FOR_JASH.md); retooling scraps work in progress with no steel refund, and the panel now states the steel that will be lost before the player acts. The production panel (step 2) shows each line, its template, queue, days remaining, and steel drain, and the unit card shows unit cost; the old train buttons are gone. Line count is gated by factories in the sim since Cycle 6 step 1 (thresholds and rules below), and the panel shows the locked slots with their unlock conditions since Cycle 6 step 3 (see below). Simultaneous battle resolution shipped in Cycle 6 step 2 (see below). Cycle 6 is complete: step 4 (tests and balance) and step 5 (judge, PASS WITH FIX-NOW FLAGS, all flags closed in the step 5 commit) are done. Cycle 7 (the lore seed) is done too; see the lore seed entry under Done.

## Done since Iteration 1 (kept honest, limits noted)

- Fortifications paid in equipment (Cycle 8, all 4 steps done, judge CONDITIONAL PASS with every flag closed): buildFortification for both nations by one path, 100 equipment times the target level, 5 days, max level 2, +25 percent defense per level for units defending their own fortified province (clash defenders use the walls of the province they are entering), a named modifier in unitStats that stackPower applies once to a stack's summed defense, so combat, the AI's estimate, and the attack preview share it and every level adds exactly its stated percent; capture slights one level and loses work in progress; the Dominion walls its capital, then its Spine province facing the river, while it holds 300 equipment. On screen: the province card's level, bonus, and work in progress, a Fortify button that states its cost, days, and why it is disabled, map badges pinned above the unit chips, the preview and unit card naming the bonus, the enemy card's fortified provinces, and the Dominion's wall rule in its doctrine text. Checked by tools/fort-check.js. Capitals start unwalled until Jash rules. Limits: walls only add defense (no attrition or supply effect), the Dominion fortifies only two provinces and still piles equipment, rail starvation on the factory path is unchanged (DECISIONS item 8), and the factory badge (unlike the fort badge) still sits in the text flow where a full row of unit chips can cover it.

- Lore seed (Cycle 7, all 4 steps done, judge CONDITIONAL PASS with every flag closed): js/lore.js holds the history of the Ashwater and the Spine, both nations (about 400 words each), both capitals, and three event cards, all loop written and flagged for Jash's rewrite or veto (DECISIONS_FOR_JASH.md). The sim records three turning points in state.beats (first capital row capture, first Drone Gen 2, first line 3), once each, for the side that reached them or both; no rule reads them, and a full campaign is identical with the hook on and off. A Codex button opens the five entries and the turning points so far; a new turning point opens its card, pauses the war, says why it fired and that it changes nothing, and closing restores the player's pause. Designed first as a Claude Design canvas. Checked by tools/lore-check.js (all three cards, the codex, a same day pair, the pause restored after cards and after the codex, phone width). Limits: three cards only, none with a mechanical effect, no images or maps in the codex, a card for a line opened while paused waits for the next tick, and a turning point on the game's final tick goes to the codex list without a card. Three of the nine card texts have never fired in 1200 harness campaigns, and one cannot: the Vessari version of "The Second Generation", because research is symmetric and the Dominion researches at the first possible moment, so a player can tie it on Drone Gen 2 but never beat it.

- Balance harness (Cycle 6 step 4, ported in the reconciliation): tools/balance.js plays passive, sensible without factories (report only), sensible with factories, and reckless campaigns through public commands only, checks Jash's ruling with exit code 1 on failure, and runs seed ranges for mass testing (300 campaigns in about 2.5 seconds). Its sensible script is rebuilt from the log's definition because the Cycle 5 script was never committed. Validated against the Cycle 5 sim (commit 4909b70): passive fell on day 36 on all 8 seeds, matching the record exactly; reckless lost 8 of 8, matching the verdict; sensible won 8 of 8 with the original army at 33.3 percent, matching, but on day 25 instead of the recorded days 43 to 52, because it masses by about day 3 and pushes during the Dominion's printed 10 day mobilization. Its numbers are the baseline from Cycle 6 on.

- Simultaneous battle resolution (Cycle 6 step 2, sim plus one preview line): the movement phase now computes every engagement from a start of phase snapshot on unit copies, then applies all results at once. No nation's groups resolve first; each engagement gets its own seeded rng and canonically sorted unit lists, so reversing the unit array gives identical results (verified by the orchestrator). Opposing groups crossing the same edge in opposite directions meet in a road battle where both sides deal damage with their attack power; the winner marches on if its target is not held by staying enemy units. A province's snapshot occupants defend it even if they had orders elsewhere (their orders are cancelled for the tick), all attacking groups against one province combine into one battle, and opposing groups converging on one empty province clash there. The unit card preview keeps the province battle numbers and adds the meeting battle numbers (your attack vs their attack). The formal tests for order reversal and the edge swap plus the full balance rerun shipped in step 4 (suite 39 at the time, 42 after the reconciliation added a test that ordered defenders still defend). Both step 5 judges passed the cycle with fix-now flags; the second found that drawn in defenders depended on group sort order, fixed so every occupant of a targeted province defends it (balance byte identical, see CYCLE_LOG). The locked slot panel shipped in step 3 (see below).

- Factory gated line count (Cycle 6 step 1, sim only): each nation still starts with exactly 2 lines. Line 3 unlocks at 16 total factory levels and line 4 at 24 (one tunable config, CFG.lineUnlockFactoryLevels [0, 0, 16, 24], hard cap CFG.maxProductionLines 4). An unlock is permanent once reached. Queueing or retooling a locked line is rejected with a reason naming how many more factory levels are needed, the threshold, and the current total. The AI syncs and queues on extra lines through the identical path and costs (verified: at 16 enemy factory levels, one real tick left the AI with line 3 open and an order queued on it). The panel side shipped in step 3 (below), and the factory card states the thresholds, the live total, and permanence. Formal tests and the balance rerun shipped in step 4, which also fixed the factory path itself: with factories paid before lines it was a steel trap, and lines are now paid first. Both step 5 judges passed the cycle; their flags are closed.

## Bible v1 systems not yet started (future cycles, Jash gated)

- Navy at theater scale: coastal control, river crossings, blockade pressure on the economy.
- Air layer beyond drone wings as ground-adjacent units: interception and the drone cost-exchange scoreboard (who bleeds money faster), which is a signature mechanic and deserves its own cycle.
- EW arms race: jamming generations countering drone generations in the research tree.
- Intel and recon: fog of war, drone recon lifting it, finding soft spots.
- Production line efficiency over time (lines exist since Cycle 5 step 1 and can be retooled; output does not ramp with experience yet).
- AI rail chooser: it now lays its Spine first, then the westernmost edge, but it still does not aim at the places where its own units are starving (on passive seed 1, 11 of 14 Dominion units were penalized on day 30); one edge can still decide a passive game between day 34 and day 69.
- Lore beyond the seed: more event cards, named characters, and events with mechanical effects (the Cycle 7 cards are history only, by design).
- Multiple AI rivals and a second theater.
- Save and load, campaign scoring screen, difficulty settings.

## Explicitly out of scope for v1 (per the bible, do not build)

World map, hundreds of nations, deep diplomacy, political parties, global trade market, naval invasions, multiple playable nations.
