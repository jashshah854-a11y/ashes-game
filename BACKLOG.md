## Done since Iteration 1 (kept honest, limits noted)

- Simultaneous battle resolution (Cycle 6 step 2, sim plus one preview line): the movement phase now computes every engagement from a start of phase snapshot on unit copies, then applies all results at once. No nation's groups resolve first; each engagement gets its own seeded rng and canonically sorted unit lists, so reversing the unit array gives identical results (verified by the orchestrator). Opposing groups crossing the same edge in opposite directions meet in a road battle where both sides deal damage with their attack power; the winner marches on if its target is not held by staying enemy units. A province's snapshot occupants defend it even if they had orders elsewhere (their orders are cancelled for the tick), all attacking groups against one province combine into one battle, and opposing groups converging on one empty province clash there. The unit card preview keeps the province battle numbers and adds the meeting battle numbers (your attack vs their attack). The formal tests for order reversal and the edge swap plus the full balance rerun shipped in step 4 (suite 39, all passing; passive day 36, sensible captures 8 of 8, reckless loses 8 of 8). The step 5 judge passed the cycle with fix-now flags, all closed in the step 5 commit. The locked slot panel shipped in step 3 (see below).

- Factory gated line count (Cycle 6 step 1, sim only): each nation still starts with exactly 2 lines. Line 3 unlocks at 16 total factory levels and line 4 at 24 (one tunable config, CFG.lineUnlockFactoryLevels [0, 0, 16, 24], hard cap CFG.maxProductionLines 4). An unlock is permanent once reached. Queueing or retooling a locked line is rejected with a reason naming how many more factory levels are needed, the threshold, and the current total. The AI syncs and queues on extra lines through the identical path and costs (verified: at 16 enemy factory levels, one real tick left the AI with line 3 open and an order queued on it). The panel side shipped in step 3 (below). Formal tests and the balance rerun shipped in step 4; the step 5 judge passed the cycle and its flags are closed.
