Remembered: seen before but not today, dimmed and hatched on the map; its ghost figures and its province card are from the day marked, and may have changed since. Dark: never seen; only the map itself is known, no owner, units, factories, forts, or rail are shown. Sight comes from your provinces and the provinces next to them, your units and the provinces next to them, drone wings, which see one province further (two provinces away), and an agent, which watches its own province. Fog gates information, never orders: you may still march or attack into any province you could reach before.

Click one of your units to select it, then click a highlighted neighboring province to march or attack. Blue is yours, red is the Dominion. A star marks a capital. Take theirs before the campaign ends. Rail is supply: provinces off your rail net show hatched, and units there weaken every day. Build rail from the province card to feed new ground.

Production

Lines build one unit at a time and drain steel every day while they work. Lines are paid before factories. If the steel stockpile cannot pay a line for a day, that line stalls and makes no progress. A finished unit deploys at your capital; a finished Interceptor does not deploy, it joins your interceptor stockpile; a finished Agent does not deploy either, it joins your agent stockpile, ready to plant from a province card. A line that keeps building the same template gains efficiency on every funded working day and finishes units sooner, up to full efficiency at 150%. Retooling a line to a different template scraps the unit in progress, loses the steel already spent on it, and drops the line's efficiency back to its starting 100%. Queuing a unit also pays its manpower cost in full from your manpower pool, beside its steel cost; a queue attempt the pool cannot pay is refused, and the panel shows the reason.
